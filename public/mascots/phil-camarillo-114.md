## About

Phil the Parrot is Camarillo's longtime resident macaw. Spotters have seen him here since at least June 2023, always wearing his red Crew Member badge. He slipped away for a while in spring 2026, and Larry the Lobster moved in, but Phil is back on duty, perched high over the aisles. These days he and Larry share the store.
