## About

Kiwi the Kiwi presides over Trader Joe’s San Diego in the Carmel Valley neighborhood — a fluffy brown kiwi bird with a long pale beak, lounging on the wood shelving in a sun hat and palm-tree sunglasses. (Note: this record was previously mislabeled as Scripps Ranch — that store is #221, not #223.)
