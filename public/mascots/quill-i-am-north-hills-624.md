## About

Quill-i-am the Porcupine watches over Trader Joe’s Pittsburgh North Hills (in the McCandless area) on Covenant Ave — a fluffy brown porcupine perched on top of the in-store ‘ORGANIC’ display sign. The pun on ‘William’ is, of course, a poke at his quills.
