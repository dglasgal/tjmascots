A hidden animal "mascot" at the Trader Joe's in Lincoln, NE.

- **Animal:** Mammoth
- **Store:** Lincoln (store #722)
- **Location:** Lincoln, NE
- **Address:** 3120 Pine Lake Rd, Ste R, Lincoln, NE, 68516
- **Has photo:** not yet
- **Page:** https://tjmascots.com/mascot/archie-lincoln-722

## About

Store 722. Archie roams the Lincoln, NE store — an homage to the Archie of his namesake: the world’s largest Columbian mammoth, unearthed in western Nebraska and now on display at the University of Nebraska State Museum. A photo of our Archie is still being arranged.
