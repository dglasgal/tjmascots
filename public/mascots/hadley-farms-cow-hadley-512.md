## About

The Hadley Farms Cow stands watch at Trader Joe’s Hadley on Russell St — a fitting mascot for one of the most farm-rich towns in western Massachusetts. Hadley sits in the heart of the Pioneer Valley, long famous for its dairy farms, asparagus fields, and roadside farm stands. The local cow imagery is everywhere here, from farm-stand mascots to the Hadley Farms Meeting House bakery brand.
