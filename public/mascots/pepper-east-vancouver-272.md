## About

Pepper the Platypus won a community mascot vote at Trader Joe’s East Vancouver, Washington — and she’s now a proud mom. Caught here in her green-and-red Christmas knit sweater with her TJ ‘PEPPER — Crew Member’ badge, surrounded by her three puggles (the proper term for platypus pups). Posed for a holiday family portrait under a ‘With Your Festive Goodies’ chalkboard.
