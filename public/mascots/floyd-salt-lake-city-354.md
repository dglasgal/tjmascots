A hidden animal "mascot" at the Trader Joe's in Salt Lake City, UT.

- **Location:** Salt Lake City, UT

## About

Named for Pink Floyd, the celebrity flamingo that famously escaped from Tracy Aviary in 1987 and lived for many years on his own at the Great Salt Lake. The image shown here is the in-store artwork featuring Floyd — we don't have a proper photo of the actual mascot yet. If you spot him, please upload a better photo!
