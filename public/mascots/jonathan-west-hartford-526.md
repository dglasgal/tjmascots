## About

Jonathan the Husky guards Trader Joe’s West Hartford on New Britain Ave — named (we’re guessing) for the University of Connecticut’s beloved live mascot. UConn has had a husky named Jonathan as its official mascot since 1934, currently on Jonathan XV; the name honors Connecticut’s Revolutionary-era governor Jonathan Trumbull. UConn’s Storrs campus is about a 30-minute drive from the store. Caught here lounging on top of a row of pasta sauce jars in classic gray-and-white husky coat.
