## About

Mochi and Pickles are the duo at Trader Joe's Danbury, Connecticut. Mochi is the teal-and-white one (left), and Pickles is the green-striped one (right) — caught here in his Santa suit and Trader Joe's name tag. (Schmoopy, a previous Danbury mascot, has retired.)
