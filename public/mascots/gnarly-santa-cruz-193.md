## About

Gnarly the Narwhal rides at Trader Joe's Santa Cruz on Front Street — the name is perfect surf-town slang for the legendary breaks just down the hill at Steamer Lane. Blue body, signature white spiral tusk, with a green knitted wave-cap, caught here lounging among autumn leaves with a painted Santa Cruz beach scene behind him.
