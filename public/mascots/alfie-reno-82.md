## About

Alfie the Wolf howls over the Reno Trader Joe's — a tribute to the University of Nevada's Wolf Pack, which he proudly reps in his navy 'NEVADA' tee. Caught here on a Thanksgiving display with a turkey friend on a bale of hay.
