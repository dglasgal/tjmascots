## About

Nutmeg the Groundhog is the resident mascot at Trader Joe's Elkridge in Howard County, Maryland (the Columbia area). Caught here in his Santa hat, holding a snowball, watching over a freeze-dried strawberries shelf-tag. Spotters: if his crew-member badge says something other than 'Nutmeg', let us know!
