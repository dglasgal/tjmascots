## About

Mocha the Mare watches over Trader Joe's Wellington in South Florida — chocolate-brown with a black mane and a white star on her forehead. Caught here perched in a basket alongside the Babybel cheese rounds. Often spotted in seasonal getups, like her signature orange Halloween tutu.
