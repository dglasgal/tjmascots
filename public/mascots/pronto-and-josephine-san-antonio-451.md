## About

Pronto and Josephine are the longhorn bull duo at Trader Joe's Alamo Heights in San Antonio. Pronto's the brown one in the blue button-down and plaid shorts, perched on the Snack Bar canisters by the Seasoning Greetings chalkboard. Josephine — white-and-brown, slightly more elegant — keeps watch from her perch in front of a colorful Mexican serape blanket on the back wall.
