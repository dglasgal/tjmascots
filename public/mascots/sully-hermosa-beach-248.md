## About

Sully the seal lives at Trader Joe's Hermosa Beach, just steps from the South Bay coast on Pacific Coast Highway. Real seals (and sometimes sea lions) have been spotted near the store — these marine mammals occasionally venture into the coastal commercial areas near the beach, with sightings reported as recently as March 2026. The crew dresses Sully up for holidays — he's been seen in his Valentine's Day best.
