## About

Clover the Cow holds court at Trader Joe's Austin Great Hills (the Arboretum area) — a black-and-white spotted cow with her Trader Joe's name tag, posted up among the Petite Pumpkin Spice Cookies and Pumpkin Spice Cold Brew Coffee on the seasonal shelf.
