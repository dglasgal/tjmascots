## About

Sparky the Sun Devil reigns over Trader Joe's South Tempe — a tribute to nearby Arizona State University, where he's been the official mascot since 1946. Decked out in ASU maroon-and-gold with his signature yellow horns, mischievous grin, and yellow trident in hand. Caught here perched next to a six-pack of Stockyard Oatmeal Stout.
