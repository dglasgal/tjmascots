## About

Peppa is the resident pig at Trader Joe's Arlington VA on N Highland St — a card-carrying crew member with her own Trader Joe's name tag. Caught here during the Thanksgiving rush, perched on a stack of Cornbread Stuffing Mix boxes between rows of Fried Onions.
