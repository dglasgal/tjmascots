## About

Javi is the resident javelina at Trader Joe's Gilbert — Arizona's third javelina mascot, joining his counterparts at the Glendale store and other AZ TJ's. (The store's signage notes that Rocky the Roadrunner was the previous Gilbert mascot before he retired.) Caught here keeping watch over the wine crate.
