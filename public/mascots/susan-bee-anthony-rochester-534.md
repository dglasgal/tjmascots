## About

Susan Bee Anthony presides over Trader Joe's Rochester, New York on Monroe Ave — a yellow-and-black bee dressed in honor of suffragist Susan B. Anthony, who lived in Rochester for nearly her entire adult life and is buried at Mount Hope Cemetery just down the road. Caught here perched in a wrapped Panettone gift box with her Trader Joe's name tag.
