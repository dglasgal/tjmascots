## About

The mysterious resident of Trader Joe's Glastonbury, Connecticut — a green creature with a flashy red-and-yellow neck frill, sharp black claws, and a fierce expression. Could be a frilled lizard, could be a baby dragon, could be something else entirely. Spotters: please proceed with caution if you try to get close enough for a photo, and if you can, ask the store's mascot handler what kind of animal he is — and submit his name too while you're at it.
