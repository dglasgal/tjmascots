## About

Animal the Muppet is the mascot at Trader Joe's West Hollywood on Sunset Blvd — the store runs a kids' scavenger hunt: find Animal hiding somewhere in the aisles and win a treat. The image shown is the in-store sign painted by the crew ("We've drummed up some fun!!"). If you can find Animal himself, please upload a photo so we can put a real face to the name!
