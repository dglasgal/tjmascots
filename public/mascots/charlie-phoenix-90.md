## About

Charlie the Camel lives at Trader Joe's Phoenix Biltmore — named for nearby Camelback Road and Camelback Mountain. The store runs a kids' scavenger hunt: find Charlie hiding in the aisles and claim a prize at the register. Caught here peeking up below the official painted sign that introduces him.
