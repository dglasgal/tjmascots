## About

Ollie the Octopus presides over Trader Joe's Long Beach (Bellflower) in his trademark red shades and 'OLLIE — CREW MEMBER' badge, perched on a wooden lobster-trap display. He has a lobster friend he rescued from the local seafood section, who now keeps him company up on the trap.
