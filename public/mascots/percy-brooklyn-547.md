## About

Percy the Parrot rules over Trader Joe's Downtown Brooklyn on Gold Street — one of the busiest Trader Joe's stores in the entire country. Per crew scuttlebutt, NYC is in a league of its own by volume nationally, and within NYC three stores are in a constant photo-finish for #1: 72nd Street, Union Square, and Downtown Brooklyn. So Percy works one of the busiest registers in America. Caught here in his blue tropical Hawaiian shirt and a teal-and-gold birthday party hat, celebrating the team captain's birthday — TJ name tag and all.
