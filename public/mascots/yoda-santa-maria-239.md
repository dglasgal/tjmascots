## About

Yoda is the resident Jedi Master at Trader Joe's Santa Maria — a card-carrying crew member with his own Trader Joe's name tag. (Species unknown — Yoda's kind is one of Star Wars' great mysteries.) Caught here in his Jedi robes, lightsaber traded for a pack of Spearmint Zero Sugar gum.
