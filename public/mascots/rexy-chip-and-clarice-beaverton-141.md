## About

Rexy, Chip and Clarice are the three-mascot team at Trader Joe's Beaverton Town Center, Oregon — Rexy the dog (left), Chip the chipmunk (center, in his wicker basket), and Clarice the sheep (right). All three are hand-painted cardboard cut-outs in front of a forest scene above the dairy aisle. Beaverton is one of the rare TJ's stores with a triple mascot.
