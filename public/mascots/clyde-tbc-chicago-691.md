## About

The resident lion at Trader Joe's Chicago Clybourn Corridor on N Clybourn Ave — sporting a magnificent orange mane and a quiet majesty befitting a city whose most famous statues are the bronze lions guarding the Art Institute of Chicago. We think his name is 'Clyde' (a fitting tip-of-the-paw to Clybourn Ave), but it's not yet confirmed — spotters: please check his Trader Joe's crew member badge and submit his actual name!
