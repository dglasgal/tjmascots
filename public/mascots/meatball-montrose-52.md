## About

Named for Meatball the Bear, the celebrity black bear who became a local folk hero in 2012 after wandering down from the Angeles National Forest into Glendale-area backyards (and reportedly stealing a few frozen meatballs). The real Meatball was relocated to a sanctuary; the mascot lives on at Trader Joe's Montrose.
