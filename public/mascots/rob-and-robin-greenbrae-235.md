## About

Rob and Robin are the owl duo at Trader Joe's Greenbrae in Marin County. Rob (left) is the small fluffy one tucked next to a giant 'S' on the wall; Robin (right) is the larger spotted owl perched on the Matcha Green Tea Latte shelf. Both are card-carrying crew members.
