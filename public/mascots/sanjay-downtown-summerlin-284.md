## About

Store 284. Sanjay the Seahorse took over Downtown Summerlin from Flash the Sloth in late 2024 — a swirly teal-and-purple felt seahorse with a turquoise fin-mohawk, usually found keeping watch over the reusable produce bags in the organic section.
