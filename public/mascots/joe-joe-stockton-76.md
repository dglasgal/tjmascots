## About

Joe-Joe the Tiger lives at Trader Joe’s Stockton on Pacific Ave — a brown-and-tan striped tiger with a blue ribbon bow on top, perched in front of a wooden ‘JOE’ sign decorated with raffia and palm fronds. His TJ name badge clearly reads ‘JOE JOE — Crew Member.’
