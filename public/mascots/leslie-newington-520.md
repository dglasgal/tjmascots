## About

Leslie the Lobster is the resident crustacean at Trader Joe's Portsmouth NH (on the Newington line at 45 Gosling Rd). The store runs a scavenger hunt — shoppers who spot Leslie hiding somewhere in the aisles can claim a prize at checkout. Image shown is the in-store sign at the entrance; an actual photo of Leslie herself would be very welcome from spotters!
