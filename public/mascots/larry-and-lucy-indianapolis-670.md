## About

Larry and Lucy are the ring-tailed lemur duo at Trader Joe's Indianapolis on W 86th St — card-carrying crew members both. This shot is from the COVID era — both are sporting face masks, posed in front of a Halloween 'Trader Joe's is all treats' chalkboard mural with a witch-hat lemur backdrop. Lucy's even rocking a brown bandana on top of her mask.
