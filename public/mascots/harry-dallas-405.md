## About

Harry the Otter is a card-carrying crew member at one of Dallas's five Trader Joe's stores — sporting a tropical aloha shirt and his own Trader Joe's name tag. We've placed him at the Inwood / Lovers Lane store as a best guess; if you shop in Dallas and know which TJ's is actually Harry's home, please submit a correction!
