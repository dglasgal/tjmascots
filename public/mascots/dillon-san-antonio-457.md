## About

Dillon the Armadillo presides over Trader Joe's San Antonio Huebner Oaks on I-10 — a perfect Texas tribute (the armadillo is the official state small mammal). Caught here in his colorful straw sombrero against a hand-painted backdrop of cactus and citrus, working the full Tex-Mex vibe.
