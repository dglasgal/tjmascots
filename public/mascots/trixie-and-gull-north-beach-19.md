## About

Trixie (left) is the resident pigeon at Trader Joe's North Beach on Bay Street in San Francisco — she took over from Patrick the Shark, who retired. Gull (right) is a hand-carved wooden seabird in a denim vest, perched above the in-store Golden Gate Bridge model.
