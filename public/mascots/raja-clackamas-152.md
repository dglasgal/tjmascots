## About

Raja the Kitten — the in-store mascot at Trader Joe’s Clackamas on SE 82nd Ave — is named after a real cat who lives nearby and stops by the store regularly. In summer and other dry-weather months you can sometimes spot the actual Raja hanging out with crew members on their breaks behind the store. Trader Joe’s Clackamas: pet-friendly in the most charming, accidental way.
