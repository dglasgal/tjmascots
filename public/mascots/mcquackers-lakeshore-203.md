## About

McQuackers hails from nearby Lake Merritt, where supervising the store is his day job. By night, he's the Mayor of the Lake — running the day-to-day business of the waterfowl and safeguarding its inhabitants. He's often seen taking breaks from the store on Friday mornings to run with the [Donut Runners](https://www.frieddoughfriday.com/), the best running group in the area.
