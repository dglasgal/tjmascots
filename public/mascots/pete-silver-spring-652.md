## About

Pete the Penguin holds court at Trader Joe's Silver Spring on Columbia Pike — a black-and-yellow king-style penguin with his Trader Joe's name tag, caught here clutching a paper-craft bouquet of pink and purple tulips against a painted ocean-and-sky mural.
