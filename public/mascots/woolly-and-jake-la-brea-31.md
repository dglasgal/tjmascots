## About

Woolly the Woolly Mammoth (left) and Jake the Shark (right) are the two-mascot team at Trader Joe's La Brea in Los Angeles — a tip of the trunk to the famous La Brea Tar Pits where mammoths once roamed, and a nod to the LA shoreline scene just down the road.
