## About

Trader Joe’s Arlington now has a duo: the unnamed lobster (niece/nephew of retired Lulu the Lobster — name still being decided by the crew) shares the spotlight with Froggie the Frog. Froggie wears a floral crew shirt and gray overalls with his TJ name badge that reads ‘FROGGIE — Crew Member.’
