## About

The wolf mascot at Trader Joe's Colonie, NY — the store is literally on Wolf Road, so a wolf mascot is on-the-nose perfect. His name is reported two different ways: the crew-member badge in this photo reads 'TJ', but he's also been called 'Wolfington' (an obvious nod to the address).
