## About

Grace the Giraffe lives at Trader Joe's Colorado Springs in University Village — named for the giraffe herd at the nearby Cheyenne Mountain Zoo. Caught here decked out for St. Patrick's Day in a green leprechaun top hat with a gold buckle, sporting her TJ name badge. (She's got a sister giraffe on the in-store sign.)
