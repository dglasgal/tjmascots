## About

Forrest the Buck stands guard at Trader Joe's Rochester Hills, Michigan on Walton Blvd — a perfect match of name and habitat (a 'Forrest' deer in suburban Oakland County, peak Michigan whitetail country). Caught here in his yellow scarf and red plaid Buffalo-check, with his Trader Joe's name tag, holding up a kid joke whiteboard: "What tree fits in your hand? A Palm Tree." Perched on top of a row of wine bottles.
