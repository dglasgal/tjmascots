A hidden animal "mascot" at the Trader Joe's in Mt. Pleasant, SC.

- **Name:** Lucy and Sullivan
- **Animal:** Dolphin
- **Store:** Mt. Pleasant (store #812)
- **Location:** Mt. Pleasant, SC
- **Address:** 1909 Hwy 17 N, Mt. Pleasant, SC, 29464
- **Has photo:** yes
- **Photo:** https://tjmascots.com/photos/490.jpg

## About

Dolphins live in nearby Shem Creek and are often spotted shopping for shrimp and other fish at this Trader Joes. they are usually wearing overcoats and in disguise.
