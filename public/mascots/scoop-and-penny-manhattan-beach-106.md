## About

Scoop and Penny are the pelican duo at Trader Joe's Manhattan Beach on Rosecrans — Scoop perches up front by the wipes display while Penny watches over the aisles from above. Card-carrying crew members both, just blocks from the Pacific where their real-life cousins dive for fish off the South Bay coast.
