## About

Bridget the Troll presides over the 59th Street Bridgemarket Trader Joe’s in Manhattan, tucked beneath the Queensboro Bridge — a pun so good it hurts. Caught here as a pink-haired troll with a teal floral headband, in a flowery dress with a red TJ price-tag tied around her wrist.
