## About

Lil' Rocky is the resident plush rock at Trader Joe's Little Rock, Arkansas — a perfect punny tribute to the city. A cheerful gray rock with a wild tuft of white hair, posed here on a watermelon-themed display with his Trader Joe's name tag.
