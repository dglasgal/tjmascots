## About

Brioche and Toasty are the toast-shaped duo at Trader Joe's El Cerrito — the store used to have a lobster mascot before this pair took over. Caught here is Toasty alone, a smiling piece of toast with his Trader Joe's name tag, perched atop the brass entrance bell. Spotters: Brioche is still out there somewhere — please snap a photo of Toasty's buddy and submit it!
