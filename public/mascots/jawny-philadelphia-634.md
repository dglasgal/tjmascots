## About

Jawny the Raccoon presides over Trader Joe's Philadelphia (Rittenhouse) on Market Street — the name alone is classic Philly slang. Caught here perched on the wooden crates next to a can of Quinoa Dolmas, sporting his Trader Joe's crew member badge.
