## About

Ally the Alpaca holds court at Trader Joe’s Yorktown on Crompond Road in northern Westchester County — a fluffy cream alpaca with a knitted red pom-pom hat, a matching red-and-cream knit scarf, and a tiny pair of round dark sunglasses, perched on a wood counter under the store’s ‘Stop By and Say Hi!’ chalkboard.
