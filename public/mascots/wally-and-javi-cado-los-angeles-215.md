## About

Wally and Javi Cado are the duo at Trader Joe's Sawtelle / West LA — Wally the walrus (left) is often spotted napping on top of the shelves with his trademark tusks, and Javi Cado (right) is a fuzzy green avocado with a smiling brown pit-face. They keep each other company watching over the Olympic Blvd store.
