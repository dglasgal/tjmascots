## About

A two-mascot crew at Trader Joe’s Shoreview, Minnesota. Garbanzo the Red Fox is decked out in his brown cowboy hat and red bandana with his TJ name tag. Joining him: Melvin the Horse — a brown plush horse with a tan mane and a matching red bandana, name badge reading ‘MELVIN — Crew Member,’ caught here lounging on a wooden display by an old brass bell.
