## About

Isla is the current mascot at Trader Joe's Goleta — a Channel Islands fox, named for the Channel Islands National Park visible from the Santa Barbara coast. The Channel Islands fox is endemic to the islands offshore and an iconic Southern California species. Caught here with her TJ crew member badge in front of a colorful orange-and-purple painted backdrop. Replaced Wally the Walrus (now retired).
