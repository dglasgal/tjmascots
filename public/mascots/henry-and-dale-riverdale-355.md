## About

Henry and Dale are the resident duo at Trader Joe's Riverdale, Utah's northernmost TJ's. Dale is an otter; Henry's species hasn't been confirmed yet — if you shop here, please submit a photo of Henry (and his animal type) so we can complete the picture!
